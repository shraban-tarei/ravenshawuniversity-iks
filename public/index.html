01 / THE KNOWLEDGE TRADITION

Rooted in wisdom.
Open to the world.

Indian Knowledge Systems (IKS) bring together the intellectual, scientific, artistic and philosophical traditions that have evolved across the Indian subcontinent. From mathematics, astronomy, medicine and ecology to language, literature, architecture, music and ethical thought, these traditions invite us to study knowledge in context and connect inherited insight with contemporary inquiry.

This portal celebrates a living conversation between classical learning and modern education—encouraging students to question, document, preserve and apply knowledge with curiosity, evidence and responsibility.

“Jñānameva śaktiḥ” Knowledge itself is power.
02 / A POEM IN TIME

More than years.
A living story.

When Odisha sought the light of learning,
a small beginning answered the call.
In eighteen sixty-eight, a seed was planted,
with hope enough to outgrow every wall.

From Cuttack's early classrooms onward,
through changing times, the red walls rose;
each generation brought a question,
each open book, a path to compose.

Through struggle, thought and public service,
its halls heard history find its voice.
Today the college stands a university—
where memory meets tomorrow's choice.
